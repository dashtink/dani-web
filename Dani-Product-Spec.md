### My Philosophy

Here's what I've learned: crafting your story is key. You can build the most sophisticated marketing infrastructure in the world, but if you're telling the wrong story, none of it matters. Finding that perfect message that makes someone stop scrolling and think "wait, this is exactly what I need" is what gets me up in the morning.

But knowing your story is just the beginning. Once you nail it, that narrative has to flow seamlessly into everything: product strategy, content, sales conversations, campaigns. And you can't get complacent—you need to keep learning and iterating as your product and the market grow.

I'm constantly experimenting with new tools and approaches—whether that's building apps I wish existed, running a home server, or architecting AI workflows that cut content creation time by hours. ### What I Care About

Too many Product Marketers forget about the "Product" in their title. I don't. I've worked at companies where product actually means something—where the technology is complex and the buyers are technical. I'm not just repackaging what Product hands me. I'm in the room when specs are being written, asking questions that surface whether we're solving a real problem or just building something cool.

I don't do corporate jargon. I write and speak like a human, which means messaging that sales teams actually use and prospects actually read.

I care about sustainability and building things that last. I've seen too many companies chase growth at all costs and burn out their teams. I'm interested in companies with strong product-market fit that are building for the long term, not just the next funding round.
